Your account

Delete your online account

For the game Grim Jr. Rise!, by ORYN LLC (trading as ORY Studios).

  • Still have the game? Open Settings and tap Delete my online account. It is deleted at once.
  • Removed the game? Email us your account code and we delete it for you within 30 days.
  • Your game on your phone is not touched. Only what is stored online is deleted.

From: HR. Shredded, not filed.

If you no longer have the game

Write to support@orynn.io with the subject "Delete my Grim Jr. online account" and your account code. It looks like GRIM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX and is shown in the game under Settings, next to "Delete my online account". If you did not keep it, tell us your display name, your rank and roughly when you last played; we delete an account only when those match exactly one player.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to say it is done. We do not ask for your name, and we keep no copy of your email beyond answering you.

What is deleted

Your anonymous account and Unity's sign-in record for it; your saved game on our servers and its backup; your display name; your character's card that other players fight; and your place, points, tickets and rewards in the Reaper Rankings.

What is kept, and for how long

The results of fights already fought stay in that week's Rankings log, shown as "Someone who has left", and are removed with the rest of that week about eight weeks later. Nothing else is kept.
