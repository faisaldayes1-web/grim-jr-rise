Privacy

Privacy policy

For the game Grim Jr. Rise! and this website. Written plainly, on purpose.

Effective date: 13 November 2026.

  • The game collects nothing until you play online. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. If you use online play (the Reaper Rankings and cloud save), the game makes an anonymous account for you and stores your saved game, a "Reaper" number as your name and your Rankings results on our servers. You can delete all of it in Settings at any time.
  • This website collects nothing either, unless you sign up for the opening-day email. Then your email address goes to a Google Form, and we use it for two emails only: a thank-you when you sign up, and one to tell you the game is out.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

From: HR. Filed in triplicate.

Who we are

Grim Jr. Rise! and this website are made and run by ORYN LLC, a California limited liability company trading as ORY Studios ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@orynn.io.

The game

What stays on your phone

The game keeps one save file on your device. It holds your progress (chapters, days, gear, spirits and currencies earned in the game), your settings (such as sound and speed) and the name on Grim Jr.'s employee badge, if you change it from "Intern". The badge name is shown only on your own screen. It is not your online display name, which is described under Online play.

The game reads your device's clock to work out things like how long the hot spring has been brewing while you were away. The time is not sent anywhere.

Unless you use online play, the save file is never sent to us and we cannot see it. If you delete the game, the file on your phone is deleted with it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include it in your personal backup, under your own account and Apple's or Google's rules; we never receive it.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in, no password and no email address. Online play uses an anonymous account the game makes for you.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No chat, no messages and no friends lists. In the online Reaper Rankings your rivals are other players' characters, and some computer-controlled ones.
  • It does not need the internet to play. Only online play and cloud save use it.

Online play

Online play is your choice. The game plays in full without it.

If you use the online Reaper Rankings or cloud save, the game creates an anonymous account for you. It does not ask for your name, email or phone number. We store: a random account number; your saved game; a display name ("Reaper" and a number; choosing your own name is not open yet); and your character's level and fighting numbers, your rank, points and rewards in the Rankings, and the week's record of fights in your group.

Other players can see your display name, your character's level and power, and your place in the Rankings. They can fight a computer-run copy of your character while you are away. There is no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

This data is kept on servers run for us by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services), which may be outside your country. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising. Like any internet service, the servers see the network address your phone connects from; we do not store it with your account.

We keep this data until you delete your online account. When a newer saved game replaces an older one, the older one is kept for 30 days as a backup and then deleted. A week's record of fights is deleted about eight weeks after that week ends.

You can delete your online account and everything stored with it at any time from Settings > Delete my online account. Your game on your phone is not affected. If you no longer have the game, follow the steps on our account deletion page or write to support@orynn.io, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Some seats in the Rankings are filled by computer-controlled rivals.

Purchases

There is nothing to buy. The game is free, and real-money purchases are switched off: there is no payment system in the game and nobody is charged. The shop only offers things you get with in-game currency you earn by playing.

Children

This game is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country if it is higher). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an online account, write to support@orynn.io and we will delete it. There are no ads and no way for players to talk to each other.

This website

What we do not do

This website sets no cookies, uses no trackers or analytics, and loads nothing from other sites. The home page has one small script that moves the screenshot carousel; it stores nothing. We do not use server records to identify or follow visitors.

The opening-day email

If you press "Sign up for the memo", a Google Form opens in a new tab. If you send it, your email address is stored in that form, which is run from ORY Studios' own Google account. We use it for two things: a thank-you email when you sign up, and one email when the game opens. We will not use it for anything else, and we will not sell it or give it to anyone.

Google runs the form, so Google's privacy policy also applies while you are on it. To have your address removed at any time, write to support@orynn.io. After the opening-day email has gone out, we delete the list.

Links to other sites

This website links to YouTube and to the Google Form. When you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Emailing us

If you write to support@orynn.io, we have your email address and whatever you send us. We use it only to answer you, and keep it only as long as we need to help.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@orynn.io. In practice the only things we could hold are your email address, if you signed up or wrote to us, and your online account, if you made one. You can delete the online account yourself in the game's Settings. We will answer any request within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the UK or the European Union), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Changes

If what the game or this website collects ever changes, we will update this page before that change goes live and change the date at the top.
