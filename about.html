The job

Somebody has to be Death.

With Death at the shop, nobody has been reaping. The queue at the exit has stopped and the dead are getting comfortable. The scythe may only be held by a Grim. There is one other Grim. You are his thumb.

  1. Clock in

    Start a shift from the Breakroom. He walks, the days tick by, and from Day 6 things start wanting a word.

  2. Build a run

    Draft skills as you level. Frost, shadow, bone, toxic or holy: no two shifts play the same.

  3. Reap or spare

    A knight who misses his family. A nervous wisp. A customer who wants the manager. The job says reap. It is your call.

  4. Hit the wall

    Around Day 35 something much bigger than him is waiting. You will lose. That is the plan.

  5. Train, then rise

    Take the loot home. Forge, merge, train, soak in the hot spring. Go back and win.

How we got here

Gone for milk. Back in 5 min.

For as long as there has been an ending, one person has handled it. Death did every job himself and never took a day off.

Then one evening he looked in the fridge, put on his good hood, and stuck a note on his office door. That was five hours ago. HR will not say where he went. HR has laminated the note.

With nobody reaping, the queue at the exit stopped. The dead stood about, got bored, got jobs, and got in the way. They appealed. They complained. One of them made a sign.

HR read the rulebook. The scythe may only be held by a Grim. HR checked the files. There is exactly one other Grim. He was found on a chair outside his father's office, holding a clean glass, waiting.

HR cannot make a child Death. There are forms. So his badge says Intern (Acting Death), the scythe is still set to his father's height, and his robe has already been patched twice. He is not worried. The shop must be far.

Frequently filed questions

FAQ

What kind of game is it?

An idle adventure roguelike you play in portrait with one thumb. Grim Jr. walks and fights on his own; you choose his skills, decide who to reap or spare, and build him up between shifts.

When does it come out, and on what?

No date yet. It is coming to the App Store for iPhone, and to Android. Leave your email below and you get one message when the doors open.

How much will it cost?

Not announced. The build in development has every real-money button switched off.

Is it scary or sad?

Neither. It is cute-spooky, and he is not worried about Dad at all. Nobody is hurt: everyone he meets is already dead and merely overdue. Defeated clients turn into a little soul that floats into his lantern.

Why does he have no mouth?

Because he does not need one. He says everything with his eyes, his hood and a small hop. It is the one rule nobody is allowed to break, including us.

Is he Death's son?

Yes. Dad went out for milk and left the scythe by the door.

Where is Death?

At the shop. He will be back in five minutes. That is what the note says and HR has no reason to doubt the note.

Who is making it?

ORY Studios. A very small team. Roughly intern-sized.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Email support@orynn.io. Tell us your device and what happened. Never send a password or payment details.
